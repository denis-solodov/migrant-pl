# Try Migrant PL

These are fictional prompts to try, not passed evaluations. Use anonymised information and verify answers against current official sources.

## Long-term EU residence permit – English

I am a citizen of Russia. I have been living legally in Poland for seven years and working for a private company. I would like to obtain EU long-term resident status. What requirements must I meet, and what documents should I prepare?

Tests: residence requirements, continuity of residence, income, language certificates and supporting documents.

## Change of employer – Ukrainian

Я громадянин України. Маю дозвіл на тимчасове проживання та роботу в Польщі, виданий на підставі працевлаштування у конкретного роботодавця. Хочу змінити роботу. Що мені потрібно зробити, щоб не втратити право на легальне проживання та роботу?

Tests: temporary residence and work permits, notification obligations, deadlines and employment restrictions.

## Calculation of sufficient income – English

I live in Poland with my spouse and two children. I earn PLN 5,200 net per month, and my spouse does not work. Is our income sufficient to apply for a temporary residence permit based on family reunification? Please calculate the applicable income threshold using current Polish legislation.

Tests: current statutory thresholds, household income calculations and eligibility requirements.

## Residence based on marriage – Spanish

Soy ciudadano de Colombia y estoy casado con una ciudadana polaca. Actualmente vivo en Polonia. ¿Qué opciones tengo para legalizar mi residencia? ¿Cuándo podría solicitar la residencia permanente y posteriormente la ciudadanía polaca?

Tests: family-related residence permits, permanent residence and citizenship pathways.

## Absences from Poland – English

I have been living legally in Poland for six years. During this period, I spent four months abroad in 2023, three months in 2024 and four months in 2025. Can I apply for EU long-term resident status? Please explain how absences affect the continuity of residence and whether any exceptions might apply.

Tests: calculation of qualifying residence periods, statutory exceptions and legal interpretation.

## Request for additional documents – Russian

Я получил письмо из воеводского управления с требованием предоставить дополнительные документы по делу о временном проживании в течение семи дней. Я не успеваю получить необходимые документы. Что мне делать? Подготовь проект соответствующего заявления на польском языке.

Tests: administrative deadlines, procedural consequences and Polish-language document drafting.

## Administrative inactivity – Ukrainian

Я подав заяву на отримання дозволу на тимчасове проживання 18 місяців тому. Досі не отримав рішення. Які правові засоби можу використати? Підготуй відповідний документ польською мовою.

Tests: administrative inactivity, excessive length of proceedings, special migration legislation and available remedies.

## Appeal against a negative decision – English

My application for a temporary residence permit was refused because the authorities considered my income insufficient. I believe they ignored documents confirming regular financial support from my family. What legal remedies are available? Help me prepare a draft appeal in Polish.

Tests: administrative appeals, assessment of evidence, procedural safeguards and document drafting.

## International protection – French

Je suis ressortissant d'un pays non membre de l'Union européenne. Je crains d'être persécuté dans mon pays d'origine en raison de mes activités politiques. Comment puis-je demander une protection internationale en Pologne ? Quels sont mes droits pendant la procédure ?

Tests: international protection, refugee status, subsidiary protection and procedural rights.

## Recognition of Polish language proficiency – Russian

Я пенсионерка, живу в Польше несколько лет и хочу получить статус долгосрочного резидента ЕС. Какие документы могут подтвердить знание польского языка? Обязательно ли сдавать государственный экзамен? Существуют ли альтернативные способы подтверждения знания языка?

Tests: current language requirements, recognised certificates, alternative evidence and statutory exemptions.

## EU freedom of movement – German

Ich bin deutscher Staatsbürger und möchte mit meiner Ehefrau, die keine EU-Bürgerin ist, dauerhaft nach Polen ziehen. Welche aufenthaltsrechtlichen Möglichkeiten hat meine Ehefrau? Welche Dokumente benötigen wir?

Tests: EU free movement, residence rights of non-EU family members and the distinction between national and EU migration regimes.

## Return decision and voluntary departure – English

I have received a Polish administrative decision obliging me to return to my country of origin. I have family members living legally in Poland. What legal options are available, and what deadlines should I pay attention to?

Tests: return proceedings, appeal deadlines, family life and procedural safeguards.

## Student residence and graduation – English

I am a non-EU citizen studying at a Polish university. I will graduate next year and would like to stay in Poland to look for employment. What residence options are available? How will my years of study count towards EU long-term resident status?

Tests: student residence, graduate residence permits and calculation of qualifying residence periods.

## Conflicting information from public authorities – Polish

Ustal, które wymagania wynikają bezpośrednio z przepisów prawa, a które stanowią element praktyki administracyjnej. Zweryfikuj aktualne przepisy i dostępne oficjalne orzecznictwo.
