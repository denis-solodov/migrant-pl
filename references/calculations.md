# Dates and residence
Verify exact procedural or substantive rule first, including KPA art. 57, PPSA rules, special migration statutes, suspensions and transitional rules. For service determine actual legally effective date including electronic service, deemed service and representative service; do not use letter issue date by default.

Use `python scripts/calculators.py deadline --start YYYY-MM-DD --amount N --unit days|weeks|months|years` for ordinary exclude-start day counting and calendar anniversary periods. Add `--roll` only if verified rule shifts end dates from Saturday or public holiday; script requires `--holidays-verified` and explicit `--holiday YYYY-MM-DD` values for relevant dates (including consecutive holidays). Do not assume substantive residence/visa expiry dates roll forward. Verify leap-day rules, exceptional suspensions and holiday list for year in question; script does not retrieve legal rules. A deadline calculation is not a conclusion about admissibility or valid filing method.

Use `schengen --on DATE --stay START:END` repeatedly for inclusive presence within rolling 180 calendar days. Include all relevant Schengen stays; merge overlaps, include entry and exit, exclude only periods legally excluded after checking visa/permit and special rules. Verify future plan day by day if needed. Output counts as of selected day only; a remaining-day count is not a guaranteed future continuous stay. No automatic extension or travel permission.

Use `period --stay START:END` for inclusive, merged calendar-day counting of documented intervals. This does not determine eligible residence for any permit. Verify weighting (e.g. study), absence limits, continuity, excluded periods, statutory calendar-year rules and special exceptions separately. Do not substitute 365-day years for calendar anniversary tests. Document disputed intervals and evaluate alternative scenarios.
