# Route selection
Check the current versions of the Ustawa o cudzoziemcach, Ustawa o obywatelstwie polskim, Kodeks postepowania administracyjnego, Prawo o postepowaniu przed sadami administracyjnymi, Ustawa o udzielaniu cudzoziemcom ochrony na terytorium RP, laws governing foreign employment, EU citizens and family members, social assistance, minimum remuneration, special Ukraine legislation and implementing regulations. Use official Polish titles as retrieved, not approximate names in citations.

| Route | Issues to verify |
|---|---|
| Visa/visa-free | Nationality, visa type, territorial scope, entry and exit, 90/180, extensions |
| Temporary residence/work | Purpose, employer, remuneration, insurance, applicable exemptions, work rights separately |
| EU Blue Card | Qualification, employment, salary benchmark, mobility and current implementing rules |
| Business | Actual activity, economic criteria, alternative statutory conditions and evidence |
| Study/research | Institution, enrolment, fees, living and return resources, insurance, special rules |
| Family | Sponsor status, relationship, actual family life, resources, housing and exemptions |
| Permanent residence | Specific statutory basis (not a generic length-of-stay route), family/origin/protection grounds |
| EU long-term resident | Eligible periods, absences, exclusions, stable resources, insurance, language proof and exemptions |
| Citizenship | Recognition vs presidential grant vs other modes, periods, language, income/housing where relevant |
| EU free movement | EU/EEA/Swiss status, family rights, worker/student/self-sufficient status, registration, proportionality |
| Protection | International and temporary protection, procedural rights, admissibility and individual risk |
| Return/detention | Grounds, exceptions, remedy, suspensive effect, proportionality, alternatives and non-refoulement |
| Other grounds | Identify exact statutory route rather than inventing a generic permit |

For every route map positive and negative conditions, evidence, competent authority, filing method, fees, dates, legal effects during proceedings, work/travel consequences and remedies. Check exceptions, transitional measures and alternative routes. Do not presume all requirements apply to all routes.

For routes depending on private contractual or family financial support, also verify current Kodeks cywilny and Kodeks rodzinny i opiekuńczy as applicable; follow civil-law.md.

For employment changes, verify the current filing channel, form and responsible party separately for a change of an existing permit, a new permit application, the worker's notice to the voivode and an employer notification to the labour office. Check the date of any special regime and distinguish work authorization from residence.
