# Civil-law sources of financial support and alternative evidence
Read this for any private support arrangement, including family transfers, gratuitous annuity (renta nieodpłatna), maintenance (alimenty), donation (darowizna), and contractual commitments. Do not assume these are interchangeable or sufficient for every residence route.

## Mandatory live checks
1. Identify the exact migration provision, the financial test, eligible family members, permissible sources and relevant period. Check whether third-party support is allowed, and distinguish own income, household income and available funds. Search official administrative judgments for the same permit and source of income.
2. Open the current Kodeks cywilny (KC) in ELI/Dziennik Ustaw, including amendments and temporal applicability. For annuity check arts. 903–907, especially art. 903¹ (form), art. 906 §2 (application of donation provisions); for donation check art. 890 §§1–2, including the effect of performance on a missing notarial form and exceptions requiring special form. Check arts. 73–74 and 77²–78¹ as relevant to form, validity and evidence. Verify exact wording and relevant civil judgments before drawing conclusions. For statutory maintenance check Kodeks rodzinny i opiekuńczy and distinguish it from voluntary contractual support.
3. Analyze separately: (a) validity and enforceability of the agreement, (b) required form and legal consequences of not observing it, (c) performance already made versus future installments, including whether performance cures a form defect and its scope, (d) admissibility and weight as evidence in migration proceedings, (e) stability, regularity, sufficiency and duration of resources. Never equate notarization with proof of ongoing income or eligibility. Distinguish notarial deed (akt notarialny) from signature certification (notarialne poświadczenie podpisu). Do not assume signature certification satisfies a statutory requirement for a notarial deed.
4. Check official CBOSA decisions on the relevant financial condition and official SN/civil court decisions on the civil-law form and effects. Check migration pages of the competent voivodeship office; if useful compare other voivodeships, marking locality and date. Search in Polish, including 'renta nieodpłatna', 'umowa renty', 'art. 906', 'art. 890', 'stabilne i regularne źródło dochodu', 'dochód członka rodziny', and the specific permit. Open full decisions and official pages. Never invent frequency of practice.

## Compare applicable variants
Consider, without assuming legal sufficiency: a privately signed written annuity agreement with evidence of actual recurring payments; a notarial deed; signature certification where relevant; other documentation of actual support (bank statements, transfer confirmations, proof of payer's means, duration and commitment); maintenance under applicable family law; other lawful income or resource arrangements under the exact migration route. Distinguish agreements from supplementary evidence and alternatives that change the legal basis. Do not recommend backdating or treating a donation or a promise as automatically stable income.

Present each viable or conditional option with: legal basis; form and consequences; evidence; migration-law eligibility and limitations; documented office/court practice; costs or burdens qualitatively; practical next step. Explain why excluded options are unavailable. Rank by documented administrative/judicial acceptance or prevalence only if comparable official evidence supports it; otherwise label relative evidentiary strength as a reasoned assessment, not actual frequency. State when evidence is insufficient to rank. A notarial deed may be useful or legally required in a particular situation, but never present it as the sole route or universal obligation without verifying the applicable provisions and facts. Do not claim ordinary written form always suffices either. If the user requests a draft, offer appropriate variants and clearly identify any parts requiring a notary or individual legal review.

For tax questions about family payments, identify whether the payments are gifts, statutory maintenance or performance of a valid annuity before calculating a threshold or deadline. Verify the tax classification, aggregation period, method of valuing the right, triggering event and any notarial exception under current law. Keep already received transfers separate from a subsequently established right.
