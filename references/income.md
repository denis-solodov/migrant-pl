# Income and financial resources
Do not store statutory monetary thresholds as constants. For each case retrieve current law and applicable regulation, benchmark announcement or official statistics. Verify the relevant effective date and any historical periods. Distinguish income (dochod), revenue (przychod), remuneration (wynagrodzenie), funds (srodki finansowe) and business turnover/profit.

## Private and family support
Always read [civil-law analysis](civil-law.md) when income comes from a family member, annuity, maintenance, donation or private commitment. Verify current civil-law provisions, actual performance and route-specific treatment of third-party support. Compare relevant forms and evidentiary alternatives; never presume a notarial deed is mandatory or sufficient. Do not count a contractual promise, actual transfer and household income twice.

## Procedure
1. Identify exact permit and statutory test; determine whether a financial requirement applies at all. Do not transfer thresholds across routes.
2. Identify whether test concerns minimum gross remuneration, income calculated under social assistance rules, stable and regular income, available funds for subsistence/return, housing costs, tuition, business outcomes or another measure. Determine comparator (strictly greater, at least), currency, family/dependant definition, time unit, reference period, exemptions and permitted sources.
3. Retrieve the current threshold and provision from ELI/Dziennik Ustaw; for statistical benchmarks retrieve the specified GUS announcement. Check rates for every relevant historical period; distinguish application date from decision date where applicable. For exchange use legally specified date/source, not a generic FX estimate.
4. For each income stream verify admissibility and regularity; record period, gross amount, legally allowable deductions, excluded benefits and currency conversions. Do not automatically deduct rent, tax, insurance, maintenance or add spouse income: establish the route-specific rule. Do not convert gross to net without sufficient inputs and verified tax/contribution rules. For students check separate living, accommodation, tuition and return cost rules; for business check statutory alternatives.
5. Use scripts/calculators.py income with threshold and actual comparable amount(s) in PLN, multiplier only when law requires per-person multiplication, comparator and verified source. For period-dependent tests run each required period separately. Averages cannot replace a month-by-month condition. For multiple independent tests calculate each separately. Stability/regularity is a legal/evidentiary assessment, not numeric output.
6. Report source, legal date, family size, accepted/excluded streams, deductions, threshold, actual, margin and exact shortfall to the next grosz for strict comparison. State unresolved qualitative conditions and alternative evidence. If uncertain, show conditional scenarios without asserting compliance.

Example (fictional numbers only): `python scripts/calculators.py income --threshold 1000 --multiplier 3 --actual 3000 --comparison gt --source 'hypothetical test only' --as-of 2026-01-01` returns failure with 0.01 shortfall. Never present this as a real legal threshold.
