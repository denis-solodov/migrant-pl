# After a refusal, withdrawal or return decision

Lead with what the person can do today. Give three separate answers: (1) whether they can lawfully remain in Poland now and on what basis, (2) last day to challenge the decision, (3) last day to depart if no other basis applies. Give exact dates and days remaining only when the triggering dates and counting rules are known. If unknown, give a conditional result and ask for the issuing authority, decision type and operative wording, legally effective service date, appeal history, and any independent residence basis or return order. Request redacted text only.

## Classify the decision and calculate each clock

- Residence permit refusal, discontinuance or withdrawal: check [Act on Foreigners](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2013/1650), especially art. 108 and art. 299(6)–(7), and [KPA](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/1960/168), arts. 57–59 and 127a–130. For an ordinary first-instance refusal, verify the usual 14-day appeal from service and submission to Szef UdSC through the voivode. Explain that a timely appeal can preserve existing art. 108 protection through finality if its conditions were met. Check procedural exceptions and any waiver of appeal.
- For a final refusal, verify whether art. 299 applies. The ordinary 30-day departure period runs from finality for a first-instance decision that becomes final or from service of the final higher-authority decision where the provision so specifies. Check statutory exclusions, including prior unlawful stay when relevant, and whether art. 299(7) deems the stay lawful for departure. Check independent residence rights before concluding the person must depart. Verify whether a fresh application is barred during the departure period. Do not add the appeal and departure periods mechanically.
- International protection: identify date of original application, ordinary, accelerated, admissibility, subsequent-application, border or Dublin procedure, the actual decision and any separate return order. Check [Protection Act](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2003/1176), [EU Asylum Procedure Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1348/oj), including arts. 67–68 and transitional rules in art. 79, and applicable border return rules under [Regulation 2024/1349](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1349/oj). Verify which regime applies, appeal deadline, whether the person can remain pending appeal, any exception or required request to remain, and any departure period under art. 299(6)(1)(b) or the applicable return regime. Do not presume a 14-day appeal, automatic suspensive effect or a 30-day departure period in every protection case.
- Temporary protection, national asylum, humanitarian stay and tolerated stay: identify the precise status and remedy. Check any special or EU rules and any separately issued return decision. Distinguish a refusal to grant a status from expiry, withdrawal, refusal to accept an application and removal.
- Return order: read the operative part for voluntary departure, enforceability, territory covered and entry ban. Check current arts. 302, 315–321 and applicable EU rules. Where voluntary departure is granted, use the date stated or calculate from the verified trigger. Where no time for voluntary departure is granted, check immediate enforceability and urgent remedies. Use [official return guidance](https://mos.cudzoziemcy.gov.pl/kategorie-informacji/zobowiazanie-i-zakaz-ponownego-wjazdu/zobowiazanie-do-powrotu/).

## Identify lawful ways to remain or obtain more time

- Check a timely administrative remedy and its actual effect, and deadline restoration if admissible, including the evidence of lack of fault and simultaneous completion of the missed act. When applicable, explain the WSA complaint, normally within 30 days of service of the final decision, via the authority that issued it. A court complaint or request for suspension alone does not generally legalize stay. Check art. 61 PPSA, any special suspensive rules and what an actual suspension order does in this case. Distinguish a right to remain, suspension of enforcement and a residence title. Check work and re-entry separately.
- Check independent lawful stay, any genuinely available new application and admissibility, including application bars connected with departure or return. Avoid presenting a new filing as a way to reset time. For a return order with a voluntary departure period, verify a reasoned request to the competent Border Guard authority for a statutory extension if eligible, including evidence and when to file. A request alone does not reset the deadline. Check [MOS voluntary departure extension](https://www.mos.cudzoziemcy.gov.pl/opcje/decyzja-o-zobowiazaniu-do-powrotu/dobrowolny-wyjazd-przedluzenie/). Distinguish this from the departure period following a refusal under art. 299.
- Where removal is imminent or there is a credible risk of harm, detention, family separation or medical emergency, lead with urgent legal assistance, non-refoulement and any verified immediate procedural options. If departure is required, check [official voluntary return assistance](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/pomoc-prawna-i-dobrowolny-powrot---tlumaczenia) and relevant application deadlines.

## Explain consequences of missing the departure deadline

If there is no other lawful basis, explain the risk of unlawful stay, a return decision or enforcement of an existing decision, possible forced removal and costs, entry ban and possible SIS consequences. Verify each trigger and distinguish mandatory from discretionary consequences. Check possible fines against the actual offence and current wording (including art. 465 where applicable). Discuss detention only if statutory grounds and safeguards are relevant. Do not describe an entry ban, fine or detention as automatic merely because a permit or protection application was refused.
