What a useful answer should do

  • Verify the applicable Polish and EU legislation.
  • Use official legal texts, judgments and relevant administrative guidance.
  • Link directly to the sources used.
  • Compare relevant lawful alternatives and explain uncertainty.
  • Calculate using legally verified inputs.
  • Draft documents in Polish and explain them in your language.

Questions about Migrant PL

What is Migrant PL?

Migrant PL is an experimental open-source AI agent skill for Polish migration law. It supports source verification, evidence selection, arithmetic and document drafts in Polish.

Can I use my own language?

The skill instructs the AI agent to respond in your preferred language and draft administrative documents in Polish. Actual performance depends on the host AI model.

Does Migrant PL replace a lawyer?

No. It is an experimental information and drafting tool. Its responses may contain errors and need independent verification.

Experimental legal information tool

Migrant PL is not a substitute for professional legal advice. Responses may contain errors and should be independently verified before being used in administrative or judicial proceedings.
